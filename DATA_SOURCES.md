# MARSCOPE Dataset Register & Data Provenance Ledger

This register catalogs all planetary science datasets considered, staged, or integrated for the **MARSCOPE (Martian Surface Intelligence & Exploration System)** platform for the NASA Space Apps Challenge (*Interplanetary Survival Guide: Martian Map*).

Every planetary product is tracked by its official NASA/ESA archive identifier, mission, instrument, legal reuse status, geographic coverage status, and system integration priority.

## Open Data Compliance & Attribution Principles

1. **Strict Open-Data Compliance:** Datasets without a verified, unencumbered open license (Public Domain / CC0 / Unconditional Scientific Reuse) will not be ingested into production.
2. **Provenance Preservation:** Every derived vector or raster generated by MARSCOPE retains an immutable metadata record referencing the original PDS Product ID and spacecraft acquisition timestamp.
3. **Transparent Status Reporting:** No dataset marked as `PENDING VERIFICATION` will be advertised as fully verified until license documentation and exact bounding coordinate overlap have been inspected and confirmed.

### Prompt 5 & 6 Provenance, Schema & Rights/Retrieval Verification

- **Strict Schema Extraction:** ChemCam MOC `.LBL` (`OBJECT = FIELD` / `FIELD_NUMBER`), APXS PDS4 `.XML` (`<Field_Delimited>`, requiring `.XML` with zero silent CSV fallback), and CheMin PDS3 `.LBL`/`^STRUCTURE` `.FMT` (requiring quantitative `WEIGHT_PERCENT` unit) are parsed dynamically and fail on missing schemas, unsupported `data_type`s, unit conflicts, or mismatched headers.
- **Coordinate Semantics:** APXS carries `instrument_target_location` (target rock coordinates projected from Gale EQC); ChemCam LIBS and CheMin carry `rover_station_location` (rover traverse station joined by RMC from `MSL_waypoints.json`, with `DISTANCE_TO_TARGET` stored strictly as laser standoff and positional uncertainty kept `null` when unreported in source).
- **Uncertainty Semantics Accuracy:** APXS precision errors (`apxs_analytical_precision`) record `"unspecified_in_pds4_label"` coverage; ChemCam LIBS separates PLS model `RMSEP` (`chemcam_pls_rmsep`, `regression_model_prediction_rmsep`) from shot dispersion `SHOTS_STDEV` (`chemcam_shots_stdev`, `1_sigma_sample_stdev`); CheMin records `chemin_rietveld_2sigma` only when the companion `.LBL` `SPREADSHEET.DESCRIPTION` documents `estimated 2-sigma analytical errors` (otherwise `chemin_estimated_error_unspecified_sigma`); unrecognized CheMin phases never fall back to guessing and are rejected at Stage 5 (`UNKNOWN_SPECIES`).
- **Source-Complete Dependency Tracking & Honest Rights/Retrieval Metadata:** Reproducibility manifests track SHA-256 digests across primary data AND companion `.LBL`, `.XML`, `.FMT`, and `msl_waypoints.json` files (`dependency_artifacts`). Local artifacts without embedded XML `<Rights>`/license tags report `license_status = "not_verified"` in machine manifests (never fabricating `"Public Domain"` / `"CC0"`), and pre-existing local files report `retrieval_timestamp_utc = "unknown"` whereas files downloaded via `safe_write_downloaded_bytes` record exact UTC acquisition timestamps in `.{filename}.retrieval.json` sidecars.
- **Terrain DEM Resolution Suitability & Footprint Integrity (`mola_megt90n000cb`):** The locally verified MGS MOLA MEGDR 4 ppd product (`MEGT90N000CB.IMG` + `.LBL`, IAU 2000 Mars sphere $R = 3,396,000\text{ m}$, ~14.8 km native spacing / ~6,603 m gridded pixel spacing at Gale latitude) is explicitly classified in `/api/mobility/status` as `suitable` for `regional_elevation_analysis`, `regional_approximation_only` for `regional_roughness_analysis`, and `insufficient_resolution` (`supported = false`) for `local_slope_analysis` and `small_scale_obstacle_detection`. Requested bounding boxes smaller than `3x3` source pixels are never silently expanded (`footprint_policy = "no_expansion_v1"`).
